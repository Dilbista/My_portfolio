## 💳 Dollar Card in Nepal

While creating an AWS account, I also learned about the importance of having an international payment card.

In Nepal, I used a **Dollar Card** from a Nepali bank for the AWS registration process.

A dollar card can be used for international online payments within the applicable limits and rules.

Before getting a card, it is a good idea to check the bank's current fees, limits, and terms.

---

## 🏦 My Experience With the Bank

For my AWS learning, I used a Nepali bank's dollar card.

From my experience, I would suggest checking **Global IME Bank** if you are looking for a dollar card for international online services.

I found its charges to be cheaper for my use compared with some other options I checked.

However, bank charges and policies can change, so I recommend checking the latest fees and conditions directly with the bank before applying.

## ☁️ AWS Free Tier

During the registration process, I also paid attention to the **AWS Free Tier**.

The Free Tier provides free usage for certain AWS services within specific limits and conditions.

It is important to understand that:

- Not every AWS service is completely free.
- Free usage has specific limits.
- Some services can generate charges.
- Resources should be monitored carefully.
- Unused resources should be removed when they are no longer needed.

For a beginner like me, understanding these things is important before starting practical AWS work.
